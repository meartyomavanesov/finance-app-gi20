# Feature Requirement Specification: Savings Goals Tracking Feature

## Title: Develop Savings Goals Tracking Feature

### Description
The Savings Goals Tracking Feature allows users to set specific savings targets and monitor their progress towards achieving these goals. This feature is designed to motivate users by providing visual indicators of their savings progress and sending notifications to remind them of their goals. By tracking their savings, users can foster a sense of accountability and motivation, which are essential for improving their financial well-being.

### Functionality
1. **Goal Creation**: Users can create multiple savings goals, specifying the target amount, goal name, and deadline for each goal.
2. **Progress Tracking**: The app will display a visual progress bar or chart for each savings goal, showing how much has been saved towards the target amount.
3. **Notifications**: Users will receive notifications for:
   - Milestones achieved (e.g., reaching 25%, 50%, 75% of the goal).
   - Reminders to contribute towards their savings goals.
   - Encouragement messages to keep users motivated.
4. **Goal Editing**: Users can edit existing goals, including changing the target amount, deadline, or goal name.
5. **Goal Deletion**: Users can delete goals that are no longer relevant or needed.
6. **Integration with Budgeting**: The savings goals feature will integrate with the budgeting functionality, allowing users to allocate specific amounts from their budget towards their savings goals.
7. **Reporting**: Users will receive reports on their savings progress, including insights on how their savings habits are evolving over time.

### Requirements

#### Functional Requirements
1. **User Interface**:
   - The feature must have a user-friendly interface that allows users to easily create, view, edit, and delete savings goals.
   - Visual indicators (e.g., progress bars, charts) must be clear and easily interpretable.

2. **Goal Management**:
   - Users must be able to create a new savings goal by entering the goal name, target amount, and deadline.
   - Users must be able to view all their savings goals in a list format, with the current progress displayed for each goal.
   - Users must be able to edit existing goals, including changing the target amount, deadline, and goal name.
   - Users must be able to delete goals they no longer wish to track.

3. **Notifications**:
   - The system must send notifications to users when they reach specific milestones (e.g., 25%, 50%, 75% of the goal).
   - The system must send periodic reminders to encourage users to contribute towards their savings goals.

4. **Integration**:
   - The savings goals feature must integrate with the budgeting feature, allowing users to allocate funds from their budget towards their savings goals.

5. **Reporting**:
   - The system must generate reports that provide insights into the user's savings progress over time, including trends and patterns in their savings behavior.

#### Non-Functional Requirements
1. **Performance**:
   - The feature must load and respond to user interactions within 2 seconds.
   
2. **Usability**:
   - The interface must be intuitive and easy to navigate for users of all financial backgrounds.

3. **Security**:
   - User data related to savings goals must be securely stored and protected against unauthorized access.

4. **Compatibility**:
   - The feature must be compatible with both iOS and Android platforms, ensuring a consistent experience across devices.

5. **Scalability**:
   - The system must be able to handle a growing number of users and their respective savings goals without performance degradation.

### Conclusion
The Savings Goals Tracking Feature is a vital addition to the personal finance app, empowering users to take control of their savings and improve their financial literacy. By providing tools for goal setting, progress tracking, and motivation, this feature will enhance the overall user experience and contribute to users' financial well-being.
